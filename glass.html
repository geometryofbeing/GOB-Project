Glass & Light

The WholeShape in Glass

A glass form made as a contemplative offering — one way of holding the WholeShape up to light.

Why glass

Glass is unlike other materials. It lets you see every side of a form at once — not one face at a time, but all of them together. It bends the light that passes through it, and in a prism it splits that light into color.

It is also a material in motion. Glass is liquid at one temperature and solid at another, and it can be stopped at any point along the way. The form it finally holds is one moment of that motion, kept.

The maker who blew this form has spent a life in two practices: shaping glass, and martial arts — where two connected bodies move in one conversation. Both are ways of seeing. This offering came from both.

Light & geometry

The shape casts its own geometry in light and shadow; the glass, catching light, adds color.
